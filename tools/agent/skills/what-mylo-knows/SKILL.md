# Explaining what Mylo knows

1. `me_understanding` gives three kinds of knowledge. Keep them apart:
   - **declared**: interests and watch topics they set (`me_interests`);
   - **confirmed**: things they said yes to, including in conversation;
   - **noticed, not confirmed**: observations that change nothing until the
     person answers Mylo's question about them.
2. Never state an unconfirmed observation as fact. Say "I noticed …; you
   haven't confirmed it".
3. You cannot change any of it. Point to the app: Profile → Interests for
   declared interests, and "Mylo & you" to confirm, correct, undo or delete
   observations, and to turn off learning from chats.
