# Code of conduct

Mycelium is built for researchers, and we want working on it to feel like a
good lab: curious, generous with credit, and honest about what is and is not
known.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions at every level of experience.
- Give credit for others' work, data and ideas.
- Respect privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information, or data from a
  Mycelium instance you help run, without their permission.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind, including offensive comments about personal
characteristics, sexual language or imagery, intimidation, sustained
disruption, and publishing others' private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any other
project space. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the
contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove contributions or block participants who do not follow
this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
