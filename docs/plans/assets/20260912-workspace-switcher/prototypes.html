      All six variants have the four must-haves wired, so you are judging form, not features: keyboard switching (⌘K / Ctrl+K works in every variant), scope in the URL (watch the address bar — it is ?ws=, the Phase 3 proposal), scaling (try the 9-workspace setting), and an explicit active-workspace treatment across header, sidebar and mobile.

      A department scope shows no research feeds — switch into the department and watch the sidebar. Papers / Grants / Fellowships / Conferences are absent, not empty. That is the constraint from the plan: targets.py builds no department MatchTarget, so those feeds have nothing to serve and an always-empty entry would just teach people the product is broken.
