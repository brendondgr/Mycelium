# Literature surveys

Raw literature surveys backing `../RELATED_WORK.md`. Compiled 2026-08-06.

- **[`SYNTHESIS.md`](SYNTHESIS.md)** — start here. Cross-cuts all three surveys, compares them
  against the Mycelium stack, restates the KG-teaming hypothesis defensibly, and ranks claimable
  gaps.
- [`grants-fellowships.md`](grants-fellowships.md) — funding opportunity recommendation, eligibility
  constraints, funding datasets, evaluation pitfalls.
- [`venues-conferences.md`](venues-conferences.md) — venue/conference recommendation, journal
  finders, CFP data, deadline and acceptance-probability gaps.
- **[`interdisciplinarity-evidence.md`](interdisciplinarity-evidence.md)** — focused interrogation
  of whether team-level disciplinary complementarity helps or hurts funding. Records a correction:
  an earlier draft wrongly claimed the teaming hypothesis was contradicted, conflating
  *individual/proposal* interdisciplinarity with *team-level complementarity*. Read alongside
  `SYNTHESIS.md` §5.
- [`kg-team-formation.md`](kg-team-formation.md) — academic knowledge graphs, KG recommender
  methods, team formation, collaborator matching, science-of-science evidence on
  interdisciplinarity and funding, LLM idea generation, grant-call → team assembly.

These are surveys, not experiments — no `manifest.yaml` required. Entries that end up bearing on
a claim should be promoted into `../RELATED_WORK.md` with `[Bn]` numbering (W4).

Confidence notes carried forward from the source surveys: KG-embedding SOTA (§2.3 of
`kg-team-formation.md`) is the weakest-searched section; the exact Bromham 2016 effect magnitude
and the Lin 2023 magnitudes are paywalled and unverified; the CORDIS license string was not
rendered directly.
