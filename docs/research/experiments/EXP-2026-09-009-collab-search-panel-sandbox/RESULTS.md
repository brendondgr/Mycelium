# EXP-2026-09-009 — Results

## 1. Headline

Through the real API, 60 fake seekers made 130 searches from calls, projects
and their own words and got 476 people back with **zero** violations of shape,
privacy or refusal (H1–H3 hold); in a real browser at desktop and phone width
every entry point opens the panel, runs, and shows the groups apart (H5
holds). The test found two product bugs, both fixed: own-words search was
refused outright, and Escape could not close the panel after Run.

## 4. Interpretation

Plan CN-06's surfaces work end to end on a 600-person world: the search is
offered only where it would run (availability), the pre-filled need is
editable and sent back verbatim, nothing searches on a dry run, every result
is the coarse pointer and nothing more, and every refusal path answers as
specified. What a person *sees* matches what the API returns.

## 5. Threats to validity

- Model-dependent quality (the rerank's lines, own-words reading) was
  exercised for 10 seekers only (ISSUES 2); its quality is EXP-2026-09-008's
  and Q-047's question.
- One browser seeker and one project; the browser check proves the surfaces
  work, not that every call renders well.
- Latency reflects a shared, heavily loaded model server (ISSUES 6).

## 6. What surprised us

That the own-words path had never worked: every unit test gave it a model
reply directly, and only a run through the real reader hit the call-length
floor. And that a disabled button can strand keyboard users outside a modal.
