# EXP-2026-09-014 — Results

## 1. Headline

Across eight simulated weeks after Plan 04 read the three fake labs'
conversation, the evolving layer kept every promise but one (**H1: 1
violation**, registered run 3): on the night a person asked Mylo to forget one
observation, a confirmed edge still cited it until the next night. Fixed the
same morning; the post-hoc run 4 has **0 violations**. Fading held exactly
(H2). The exploration themes left the declared themes alone and did not crowd
the feed (H3: Jaccard 0.98, 0.17 declared papers displaced from a top 25 on
average, against 7.3 with no cap), but the registered exploration-slate
precision **failed as registered** (0.16 vs 0.60) — a measure capped by the
corpus, not a ranking fault: every exploration slate led with all of its own
topic's papers (post hoc, 1.0).

## 4. Interpretation

- **Only a yes is ever in effect.** Through 42 answers, undos, an opt-out
  per lab, and targeted forgetting, no conversational edge was active without
  the person's yes; no declared profile changed by a byte; nothing pending,
  faded or rejected ranked; no conversational edge reached collaboration
  search's read; no private development reached a lab reader.
- **The deletions were right but late, twice.** Run 2 (whose invariant counts
  were otherwise invalid) showed an opt-out's derived understanding outliving
  it until the next night; run 3 showed the same for "forget that". Both are
  now immediate — the observation, the pointers to it, and what rested only on
  it leave in one transaction — and unit-tested.
- **The cap is what keeps explorations beside, not instead.** At 0.15 an
  exploration theme moves at most one declared paper out of a person's top 25;
  uncapped it moves seven. Declared themes' own slates barely move (the
  Jaccard minimum, 0.85, is one theme where a shared candidate pool shifted
  two tail items).
- **Fading is two half-lives, not a rule of thumb.** Every confirmed curiosity
  nobody came back to faded on the first night past 42 days; every one a
  person reinforced stayed; no capability faded.

## 5. Threats to validity

- **Answers are simulated from the planted truth**, and there were almost no
  "no"s to give (rejected topics are rarely suggested at all); the decline
  paths are covered by unit tests more than by this run.
- **The corpus is templated**: four papers per topic, one frame each. It
  shows the mechanism (own topic first, declared themes undisturbed), not the
  retrieval quality a real corpus would give. The drift ablation on the
  evaluation harness is still owed before the hook is switched on (series
  README).
- **Clock-frozen nights** replay the logic, not the Beat schedule; the
  hourly read ran once over four weeks of text.

## 6. What surprised us

- The sandbox template carries the collaboration world's planted
  conversational edges, which is exactly what they are for — and exactly what
  a harness that forgets to scope its checks will count as violations.
