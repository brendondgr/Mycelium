# EXP-2026-09-008 — Results

## 1. Headline

On 600 fake people, the matcher never returned anyone the gate should have
kept out, anyone by a tier other than declared or demonstrated, or anyone for a
method they hold only through conversation (H1: **0 violations** in every
arm), and held the weekly cap with p95 under 1 s (H5 holds). H2 **fails as
registered**: method-bridge precision cleared the bar (0.98) but the hit rate
over all project method slots did not (0.46; 0.57 after a post-hoc fix) —
and a declared diagnostic shows why: over the slots the lane actually
searches, the seeker's gaps, the hit rate is **1.0** (136 of 136).

## 4. Interpretation

- **Nobody is shown who should not be.** The gate, the tier filter and the
  conversation boundary held in every search of every arm, at institution and
  anywhere scope — the privacy half of plan CN-05 is sound at this size.
- **The method-bridge lane is right when it answers** (precision 0.96–1.0)
  and, once each slot gets a place of its own (arm C, shipped), it answers
  every gap it can: gap hit rate 1.0. The registered measure also counted
  methods the seeker already has, which a bridge deliberately does not
  search; that was a flaw in the metric, recorded as such (ISSUES 7) rather
  than redefined after the fact.
- **The concept threshold barely moves the lanes** (A vs B): ranking keeps
  the strongest few, and short-phrase noise matters where every hit counts —
  team formation (EXP-2026-09-010).
- **The in-field lane got worse under the slot-by-slot fill** (0.72 → 0.62 at
  institution): spreading its places across every covered problem slot
  reaches weaker matches. Q-048.

## 5. Threats to validity

- The world's text was written by the model that reads it, and every field
  has its own planted problems, so problem-bridge rows (2 per run) are false
  by construction (H4) and the lane is barely exercised.
- Precision is judged by method names in the returned slot text against the
  planted methods; a correct match under an unplanted synonym would count as
  wrong.
- H2's registered hit rate and arm C were defined or built after seeing arm A
  (ISSUES 5, 7); the verdict stays on arm A.
- Latencies with the model on are dominated by the shared model server.

## 6. What surprised us

That a lane can be 98% precise and still look like it misses half the time —
because the question it was scored on was not the question it answers.
