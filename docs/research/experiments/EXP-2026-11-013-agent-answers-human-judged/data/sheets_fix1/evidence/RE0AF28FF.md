#4 [paper] An end-to-end machine-learning system for global eddy-resolving ocean forecast.
Authors: Zhang, Xiang, Cui, Yingzhe, Li, Furong, Zhou, Shenghui, Wu, Guangnan, Jing, Zhao, Wu, Lixin
Venue: Science advances
Year: 2026
Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43024702/
Still open/current: yes
Problem (Mycelium's reading): Machine learning ocean forecast models typically rely on initial conditions generated by traditional numerical global ocean forecast systems (GOFSs), making them unable to operate independently.
Method (Mycelium's reading): WenHai-E2E, an end-to-end machine-learning system that generates eddy-resolving global ocean forecasts directly from multisource heterogeneous ocean observations.
Setting (Mycelium's reading): Global ocean forecast
Abstract:
Machine learning (ML) models, especially those based on deep neural networks, have achieved competitive forecast accuracy and greatly reduced computational burden compared to traditional numerical global ocean forecast systems (GOFSs). However, it remains difficult for these ML models to operate on their own, as they rely on the initial conditions generated by numerical GOFSs. In this study, we present WenHai-E2E, an end-to-end ML GOFS that replaces the entire pipeline of numerical GOFSs at deployment time and makes eddy-resolving global ocean forecast directly from multisource heterogeneous ocean observations, without requiring operational initial conditions from numerical GOFSs. WenHai-E2E outperforms a state-of-the-art numerical GOFS in the forecast accuracy. Our results demonstrate the promise of ML GOFSs to surpass numerical GOFSs and provide insight into the pathway for developing next generation of GOFSs.
