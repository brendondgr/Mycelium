## Question

Who is eligible for this fellowship, and when is it due?

## Answer

The WHOI GFD Summer Graduate Research Fellowship is open to graduate students in any field who share an interest in the nonlinear dynamics of rotating, stratified fluids (including fields like oceanography, meteorology, geophysics, astrophysics, applied mathematics, engineering, and physics) #1.

It is due on February 5, 2027 #1.
