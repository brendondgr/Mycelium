## Question

What are the goals of the Marine heatwave early warning project?

## Answer

The goal of the Marine heatwave early warning project is to provide a two-to-six-week forecast of marine heatwaves for the regional coast. This is achieved using an ML-corrected ensemble and delivered to fisheries managers via a public dashboard (#1).
