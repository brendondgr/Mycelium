# R41EA2D36

## Question

What kinds of work does this call fund?

## Answer

This call funds a broad range of research and technology development, specifically:

*   **Instrumentation development:** Projects that enhance the observational, experimental, or analytical capabilities of the ocean science research community and have broad applicability (#1).
*   **Infrastructure improvements:** Specific opportunities for improvements in facilities, communications, and equipment at Marine Laboratories and Biological Field Stations (FSML), as well as the National Ocean Partnership Program (#1).
