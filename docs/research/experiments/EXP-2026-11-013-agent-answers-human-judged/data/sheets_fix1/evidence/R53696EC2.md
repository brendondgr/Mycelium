# R53696EC2

## Question

Who is eligible for this fellowship, and when is it due?

## Answer

Graduate students in any field who share a common interest in the nonlinear dynamics of rotating, stratified fluids are eligible for this fellowship, including those in fields such as physics, geophysics, astrophysics, engineering, applied mathematics, meteorology, and oceanography #1. The deadline is 2027-02-05 #1.

Abstract:
The Fulbright-Hays DDRA Fellowship Program, funded by the U.S. Department of Education (ED) and administered by the International and Foreign Language Education (IFLE) Office, provides opportunities for graduate students to conduct full-time dissertation research abroad in modern foreign languages and area studies. ED defines "area studies" as a "program of comprehensive study of the aspects of a society or societies, including the study of their geography, history, culture, economy, politics, international relations, and languages." Eligible Regions and Countries: Allowable projects are those that focus on one or more of the following geographic areas: Africa, East Asia, Southeast Asia and the Pacific Islands, South Asia, the Near East, Central and Eastern Europe and Eurasia, and the Western Hemisphere (excluding the United States and its territories). Projects Not Allowable: Applications for research focused on the following countries are not eligible: Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Cyprus, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, San Marino, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, and Vatican City. Note that it may be possible to conduct research in Western Europe, provided the project itself is focused on another world region. Projects in countries designated as "closed" by Fulbright-Hays are ineligible. See the "Maintenance Allowances" section in the FY 2025 DDRA Application Instructions for details. Applications for projects in countries currently designated by the Department of State with a Level 4 travel advisory may be submitted, but awardees may end up having their travel plans delayed or canceled due to travel restrictions. Applicants may propose research projects lasting from 6 to 12 consecutive months. Projects may begin as early as January 1, 2026 and must end by March 31, 2027. Students do not apply directly to the Department of Education for these awards. Instead, applications must be submitted on behalf of the applicant by the student's institution of higher education (IHE). Students must therefore work closely with their IHE for an application to be submitted successfully.
