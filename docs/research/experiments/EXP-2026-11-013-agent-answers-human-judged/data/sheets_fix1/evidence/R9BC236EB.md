## Question

Does this paper change anything for the Marine heatwave early warning project, and who on the team should read it?

## Answer

The paper #1 evaluates 1-14 day machine-learning forecasts for marine heatwaves in the Northern Bay of Bengal against persistence benchmarks.

**Impact on the Marine heatwave early warning project (#8):**
The project's goal is a two-to-six-week forecast using an ML-corrected ensemble. Since this paper specifically tests ML forecasts against persistence benchmarks (the same baseline your project used to show success for 2015-2024), it provides a relevant external comparison for ML-based heatwave prediction, though it focuses on a shorter timeframe (1-14 days) and a specific region (Bay of Bengal).

**Who should read it:**
*   **Tomás Ferreira-Lind (#7):** Given his interest in Physics-informed ML and his membership in the project.
*   **Sun-hee Park (#6):** As a project member and expert in ocean data tools.
*   **Mara Ellison (#3):** As the PI and lead on ocean extremes.
