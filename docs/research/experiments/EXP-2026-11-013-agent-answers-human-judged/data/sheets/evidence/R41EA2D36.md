## Question

What kinds of work does this call fund?

## Answer

This is NSF's **Ocean Technology and Interdisciplinary Coordination (OTIC)** program (#1). It funds two kinds of work:

1. **Instrumentation development** — unsolicited proposals are welcome for new instruments or technology that has broad applicability across ocean science and that enhances the observational, experimental, or analytical capabilities of the ocean research community.

2. **Facilities and infrastructure improvements** — funded through specific announcements (not open solicitation) for:
   - Improvements in Facilities, Communications, and Equipment at Biological Field Stations and Marine Laboratories (FSML)
   - The National Ocean Partnership Program

So the core of the call is **building or improving the tools and infrastructure that ocean science depends on** — new sensors, analytical methods, field-station equipment, and the like — rather than a specific science question.

The deadline is **15 February 2027** and it's still open (#1).

Given your lab's focus on ocean data assimilation and forecasting, the instrumentation-development track is the one most likely to fit — for example, if you had a new sensor or data-ingest technology that would benefit the broader ocean community.
