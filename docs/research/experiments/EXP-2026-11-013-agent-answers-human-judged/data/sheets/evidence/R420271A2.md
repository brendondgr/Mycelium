## Question

Which fellowship in our feed would suit a PhD student on the Marine heatwave early warning project?

## Answer

The best fit is the **WHOI Geophysical Fluid Dynamics (GFD) Summer Graduate Research Fellowship** (#18).

Why it suits a PhD student on Marine heatwave early warning:
- It's explicitly for **graduate students** (so it fits a PhD candidate, not a postdoc).
- It supports research in **oceanography, meteorology, applied mathematics, and physics** — all core to marine heatwave prediction and SST forecasting.
- The focus on **nonlinear dynamics of rotating, stratified fluids** aligns well with the fluid-dynamics and data-assimilation methods behind heatwave early-warning work.
- It's a **10-week residential fellowship** at Woods Hole, with supervision from WHOI staff and a lecture + written report — a strong way to deepen the project's methods and build a network.
- Deadline: **5 February 2027** (still open).

A couple of other options worth a look, depending on the student's situation:
- **NOAA Dr. Nancy Foster Scholarship** (#20) — supports independent graduate studies in oceanography (up to 4 years for doctoral students), particularly for women and members of minority groups. Deadline anticipated **October 2027**.
- **WHOI Postdoctoral Scholar Program** (#22) — only if the student has already finished their PhD; it's for new/recent PhDs, 18 months. Deadline anticipated **October 2027**.
