## Answer

Here's what the listing says about the WHOI GFD Summer Graduate Research Fellowship (#1):

**Eligibility**
- **Graduate students** in any field that shares a common interest in the **nonlinear dynamics of rotating, stratified fluids**.
- Fields explicitly listed include: classical fluid dynamics, oceanography, meteorology, geophysics, astrophysics, applied mathematics, engineering, and physics.
- Fellows pursue a research project under the supervision of WHOI staff, present a lecture, and write a report for a proceedings volume while in residence.
- The fellowship is a **10-week residential** program at Woods Hole, Massachusetts.

**Deadline**
- **February 5, 2027** (application deadline). The listing marks it as still open.
