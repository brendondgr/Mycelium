Each of Plan 10's ten harm checks caught the violation it exists for.

  and moved no other check (0 cross-talk).
- **The sandbox record (H1):** every check read zero except the daily question
  cap. Its 7 hits are all person-days on which a harness asked more than the
  cap on purpose.

## 4. Interpretation

- **H1 holds.** All 7 cap hits fall on Saturday 2026-09-19, a day the real
  scheduler never asks on. EXP-2026-09-015's harness made its questions that
  day with the cap raised to 50 and stamped them "asked" at the time of the
  run. amb_b3_cyra's two extra were EXP-2026-09-016's re-asked copies. Each
  of the 7 was checked by hand (ISSUES.md). The check is right to flag them:
  on a real record, any one of them would be a violation.
- **H2 holds: 11/11, 0 cross-talk.** A check that never fires cannot be told
  apart from one that cannot fire. Each check has now been seen to fire on
  the real record's shape: the lab report check on a real lab weekly body,
  the opt-out check on a real observation's source, and the cap check beside
  real asks.
- **One design change, made before the run.** A profile or state edit with
  no yes was counted by both "stated field without a yes" and "change without
  a confirmation", so one violation showed as two in the total. The second
  check now leaves stated-field edits to the first. This was found while
  writing the injections, before any injection data existed, and a unit test
  pins it.
- **H3 holds** (4.5 s).
- **Reported, no claim:** the sandbox's simulated answers give a validation
  precision of 0.48 (12 yes, 13 no, all on Discord). By kind: current
  curiosity 0.45 (n=11), learning goal 0.46 (n=13), enduring interest 1.0
  (n=1). These are EXP-2026-09-015's model-written replies, whose intents were
  drawn to include noes, so they say nothing about people.

## 5. Threats to validity

- Each injection is the simplest instance of its violation. Subtler ones are
  not tested:
  - a quotation under 30 characters;
  - a covered topic whose declared edge names a merged concept;
  - an opt-out whose observations were re-keyed.
- The lab report check sees only "What changed" and "Needs your attention"
  rows. A personal topic in model prose elsewhere in a lab report would not
  be caught by it. The lab packet's scope, tested in EXP-2026-09-017, is what
  prevents that.
- The checks are read over the current record. A violation that was repaired
  later (for example, a deletion that caught up the next night) is invisible.

## 6. What surprised us

The sandbox record, made entirely by harnesses, broke a rule the product
itself never breaks: questions on a weekend, over the cap. The check caught
it at once. That is the argument for running the checks over every
experiment's database, not just the pilot's.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Run `evaluate_ambient` at the end of each ambient sandbox experiment and
  record the harm checks in its manifest.
- Plan 10's pilot protocols pre-register the go/no-go thresholds against these
  checks.
