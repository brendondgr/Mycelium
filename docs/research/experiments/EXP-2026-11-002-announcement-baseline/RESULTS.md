# EXP-2026-11-002 — Results

## 1. Headline

At the committed floor, department lab-fit admitted all nine department-level
calls for PULSE Lab and three of them did not suit it (precision 0.667,
agent-labelled); no floor separates the two groups, so the hypothesis held and the
floor stays until more than one lab is labelled. The engagement measures work end
to end but read only the implementer's own test clicks, and a department's slate
was department-level for one row in thirteen.

## 4. Interpretation

- **Lab-fit (C-029).** Cosines between a lab's profile text and a call sit in a
  narrow band for bge-small, so a floor of 0.45 is not a filter at all: it
  admitted every call, including two tribal-entity training programmes and a
  landscape-architecture nomination. The ordering is still informative at the
  top (the three computing fellowships score highest), so ranking with a short
  list is defensible while a floor is not. Per the decision rule, nothing changed:
  one lab and nine calls cannot set a threshold (Q-055).
- **Department yield (Q-056).** The plan posts a department-level call only when
  it enters the department's slate. On this data the slate held one such call,
  and it had closed, so the nightly path would post nothing for this department.
  Calls exist (nine among its scored rows), but they rank below the department's
  thematic best. Whether a department should consider all its scored rows is a
  product decision, raised with the owner rather than tuned here.
- **Engagement.** Interested, Remind me, Not for us, web link clicks and route
  health are all recorded and computed; the values are test activity. Remind me
  reads zero because the one DM reminder set in Phase 4 predates the `remind`
  reaction. Route health: every announcement route in an active binding was
  verified ok.

## 5. Threats to validity

- Lab-fit labels are the agent's, one lab, nine pairs; the CGS and MAGS rows turn
  on which year's fields count (labelled by the current round).
- The department's profile and roster are simulated (FSU seed), and only one of
  its labs has a profile at all.
- Engagement numbers carry no information about researchers.
- One corpus snapshot (collected 2026-08/09).

## 6. What surprised us

How little of a department's slate is department-level: the thematic best for a
department is mostly calls its labs can take on alone, which the department
channel is designed to skip. Also: nothing in the code had ever written a lab
profile vector, so lab-fit named no lab at all until Phase 7 embedded the
profile text.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Q-055: a lab-fit floor or ranking rule, from several labs labelled by a person.
- Q-056: whether a department considers all its scored rows, not only its slate.
- Re-run `announcement_metrics` after the first month with real users; that is
  the baseline any engagement threshold should be tuned from.
