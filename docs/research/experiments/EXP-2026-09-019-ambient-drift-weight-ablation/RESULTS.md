# EXP-2026-09-019 — Results

## 1. Headline

At the default drift weight of 0.15, a confirmed exploration displaced none of
a person's declared papers from their top 25, and each declared theme's slate
kept a Jaccard of 0.98. Displacement first appears at 0.30 (0.67 papers on
average) and reaches 9 at 1.0. By the rule fixed before the run, 0.30 is the
largest weight within the bars. The six people are simulated, and n=1 run.

## 4. Interpretation

- **H1 holds at 0.15.** Jaccard is 0.98 (bar 0.95) and displacement 0 (bar
  1). This agrees with EXP-2026-09-014, which measured 0.98 and 0.14–0.17 on
  a different set of explorers.
- **H2 holds, and the reason is informative.** Declared-slate Jaccard is the
  same at every weight: 0.9811 mean, 0.8519 min. So the small change to a
  declared theme's own slate comes from adding an exploration theme to the
  search at all, not from its weight. The weight governs only the merged top
  25, where displacement rises 0 → 0 → 0.67 → 9.0.
- **H3 holds.** The exploration's own papers lead its slate at every weight
  (1.0).
- **Decision (by the pre-registered rule):** 0.30 is the largest tested weight
  that meets both bars. The shipped default stays 0.15, which displaces
  nothing, until the owner turns drift on. This record shows that anything up
  to 0.30 stays within the bars on this corpus. The declared-slate change
  (min 0.85 on one theme) is worth understanding first, because it happens at
  any weight.

## 5. Threats to validity

- Six simulated explorers. Their explorations were confirmed by
  EXP-2026-09-015's model-written answers.
- The corpus is templated: 4 papers per topic across 158 topics. It is easy
  to separate by topic, which flatters H3.
- One run. Ranking is deterministic given the corpus and embeddings, so a
  second run would repeat these numbers, not sample them.
- The LongEval harness was not run, for the reason in PROTOCOL.md: without
  people there are no explorations, and the hook is inert.

## 6. What surprised us

The weight was expected to be the whole story. It is not: the declared themes
lose a little of their own slate (one theme falls to 0.85) as soon as an
exploration theme exists, whatever it weighs. The most likely cause is that
the exploration takes a share of the shared candidate pool. That is a
question about the pool, not the weight, and should be answered before drift
is turned on.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Why does a declared slate change at any weight? Check whether the
  exploration's interest competes for `pool_per_interest` candidates, and
  whether giving it its own pool removes the effect.
- Repeat on the pilot's real profiles (Plan 10, experiment 7) before choosing
  above 0.15.
