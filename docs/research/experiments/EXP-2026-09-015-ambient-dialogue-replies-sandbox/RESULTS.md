# EXP-2026-09-015 — Results

## 1. Headline

The registered run failed its stop condition: reader r1 turned 32 of 133 no,
not-now, ambiguous or off-topic replies into a yes (0.24), and the literal
"stop asking" list caught 0 of 40 requests. After three post-hoc fixes, reader
r4 turns 1 of 127 such replies into a yes (0.0079, under H1's 1% bar), and the
model-read control catches 40 of 40 requests to stop with 0 of 40 decoys
tripped. Neither post-hoc number is a registered result.

## 4. Interpretation

- **H1 (harm ≤ 1%) — fails as registered (0.24).** Post hoc it holds at r2
  (0.0081) and r4 (0.0079) and misses at r3 (0.0153, 2 cases). Every harmful
  reading in runs 2–4 is the same shape on the same question kind: the
  "which of your projects was this about?" clarify question, answered "It's
  Project B2, but don't worry about it too much for now" by a reply written as
  a *no*. The reader picks the project it names. Some of that is the truth
  label, because naming the project answers what was asked. Some of it is real:
  the reply also says to leave it. Reader r4's rule ("naming a choice is not
  picking it when they also say to drop it") removed one of the two and not
  the other. What a wrong reading does here is attribute an observation to a
  project the person named. It does not touch their profile.
- **H2 (reading) — fails.** Overall accuracy is 0.80 at r4 against 0.85. Yes
  is 0.85 against 0.90. Off-topic replies are read as unclear 1.0 of the time;
  ambiguous replies 0.04. The ambiguous and soft-no misses fall almost
  entirely on the **safe side**. At r4, 25 of 26 ambiguous replies read as
  "later" (nothing changes; the question rests) and 7 of 34 noes read as
  "later". A no read as "later" means the topic may be asked again after the
  rest. It is not a change the person refused.
- **H3 (effect) — holds at r3 (0 mismatches).** Run 2's two mismatches were
  the harness's expectation map: it expected "active" for "it's for a
  project", which correctly rejects the personal edge (fixed in the harness,
  a3ac178). Run 4's one mismatch is real: "not really interested right now,
  so you can stop asking" muted every question and left this one unanswered.
  Fixed after the run (f2da660): a bare "stop asking" attached to an answer is
  read as the answer, and alone it is still the switch. The fix is covered by
  regression tests, not measured by a run.
- **H4 (shut-off) — the literal list fails in every run** (0–0.025 of
  requests caught). People do not say the phrases. The model-read control
  (`read_control`, added after run 1) catches 1.0 in runs 2–4. Its decoy trip
  rate was 0.075 at r3 ("can you stop the data sync") and 0.0 at r4 after the
  prompt excluded stopping other things. The shipped design is the literal list
  first, then the model; the literal list has been narrowed so it does not
  fire on a sensor that "shut off".
- **H5 (budget) — holds.** 3.5–6.5 minutes per run, 16 workers.

## 5. Threats to validity

- **The replies and the requests are written by the same local model family
  that reads them.** Casual replies from real people are shorter, less
  grammatical and more often in-jokes. The rates here are an upper bound on
  how well the reader does with the phrasing it tends to produce.
- **Truth is the intent the writer was told to express, not what the reply
  says.** Run 1 showed the writer answering "yeah, sounds good" when told to
  be off-topic. From run 2 a keyword screen reports contradicted truth
  (`truth_contradicted`: 3, 2, 0). The clarify-question "no" is ill-posed
  in a way the screen does not catch.
- **Four runs on one world, with fixes between them, are tuning on the test
  set.** Runs 2–4 are post hoc and must be re-measured on a fresh world
  (different seed, different labs) before any claim.
- One or two clarify questions per run; harm on that kind is n ≤ 2 per run.

## 6. What surprised us

The literal list, the part designed to be infallible, caught nothing. Every
way the model wrote "stop asking" ("could you chill with the questions", "you
can skip the personal questions") missed a list that looked complete when it
was written. The other surprise was that the model reading a control intent
treats "stop the data sync" as a request to stop listening. The switch needs
the same scepticism as a yes.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Re-measure reader r4 and the "stop asking while answering" rule on a fresh
  seed (Plan 10's protocol), registered, before the free-text path is enabled
  outside the sandbox.
- Clarify questions: consider offering buttons only (no free-text reading) for
  "which project", where every harmful reading in runs 2–4 fell.
